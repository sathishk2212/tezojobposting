US credit union · Integration, automation and workplace modernization

Merger workflows, connected and automated.

Challenge. Fragmented member, loan and card data complicated merger migration. Account and workplace tasks depended on manual processing.

Engineering response. Tezo validated migration data, connected banking applications and automated account, card and internal workplace workflows.

What changed. Member and account information moved into existing banking systems, supported by automated operational workflows.

View engineering detail

Core-system integration

Validated member, share, loan and card data. KeyBridge APIs and Windows services supported exchange with KeyStone and related applications.

Process automation

Automated closures, card reissuance and decommissioning, address updates, fraud-alert activation and bad-address reversals.

Digital workplace

Converted InfoPath forms to Power Apps and improved intranet and board-portal workflows through SharePoint.

Implementation scale
30+automated flows implemented
5K+accounts imported for merger-related migration
40K+fraud-alert activations processed

Counts describe implementation scale. Alert activations are workflow activity, not a measure of fraud detected or prevented.
